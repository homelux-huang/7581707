## 九題可追溯之英文時事教學例句

### 115-00-E-02｜joined

英文：More than 300 competitors joined the WorldSkills Asia event held in Taipei in November 2025.

中文：超過300位選手參加了2025年11月在臺北舉行的亞洲技能競賽。

來源：WorldSkills International，2025-12-12；[WorldSkills Asia Taipei 2025 showcases regional excellence and diversity](https://worldskills.org/media/news/worldskills-asia-taipei-2025-showcases-regional-excellence-and-diversity/)

**來源支持的事實與改寫界線**：Official report confirms more than 300 competitors took part in Taipei November 2025 event。此英文例句屬教學改寫，非原文引述。考點 joined 實際包含於英文句中。

### 115-00-E-03｜equally

英文：In September 2026, UN Women called for decision-making power to be shared equally with women.

中文：2026年9月，聯合國婦女署呼籲讓女性平等分享決策權。

來源：United Nations / UN Women，2026-09-17；[Gender Snapshot 2026 press release](https://www.un.org/sustainabledevelopment/blog/2026/09/press-release-one-in-three-institutions-built-to-advance-gender-equality-stripped-of-power-in-just-two-years-new-un-report-reveals/)

**來源支持的事實與改寫界線**：UN/UN Women calls for political and economic decision-making power to be shared more equally。此英文例句屬教學改寫，非原文引述。考點 equally 實際包含於英文句中。

### 115-00-E-06｜accurate

英文：The WMO said in October 2025 that AI could help make weather forecasts more accurate.

中文：世界氣象組織於2025年10月指出，人工智慧有助於讓天氣預報更準確。

來源：World Meteorological Organization，2025-10-24；[World Meteorological Congress endorses actions to promote AI for forecasts and warnings](https://wmo.int/media/news/world-meteorological-congress-endorses-actions-promote-ai-forecasts-and-warnings)

**來源支持的事實與改寫界線**：WMO identifies AI potential to improve accuracy, accessibility and reach of forecasts。此英文例句屬教學改寫，非原文引述。考點 accurate 實際包含於英文句中。

### 115-00-E-07｜temperature

英文：In July 2026, Taiwan's weather agency warned that the temperature could reach 38 degrees Celsius in some areas.

中文：2026年7月，臺灣氣象機關警告部分地區氣溫可能達到攝氏38度。

來源：Focus Taiwan / CNA，2026-07-02；[Heat warnings issued Thursday for most of Taiwan; south under 'red' alert](https://focustaiwan.tw/society/202607020005)

**來源支持的事實與改寫界線**：CWA July 2 warnings projected 38 degrees in selected regions; not a measurement of body temperature。此英文例句屬教學改寫，非原文引述。考點 temperature 實際包含於英文句中。

### 115-00-E-09｜choice

英文：A July 2026 UN report warned that each policy choice could affect progress toward the 2030 goals.

中文：2026年7月的聯合國報告提醒，各項政策選擇都可能影響2030年永續發展目標的進展。

來源：United Nations Statistics Division，2026-07-07；[The Sustainable Development Goals Report 2026](https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/report/2026/)

**來源支持的事實與改寫界線**：The report states choices in coming years will affect progress toward 2030 goals; singular choice is a pedagogical paraphrase。此英文例句屬教學改寫，非原文引述。考點 choice 實際包含於英文句中。

### 115-00-E-10｜deeply

英文：In October 2025, many conservationists were deeply saddened by the death of Jane Goodall.

中文：2025年10月珍古德逝世，許多保育工作者深感悲傷。

來源：Jane Goodall Institute，2025-10-10；[Dr. Jane Goodall, Renowned Ethologist, Conservationist, and Animal Behavior Expert Passes Away at Age 91](https://janegoodall.org/news/dr-jane-goodall-renowned-ethologist-conservationist-and-animal-behavior-expert-passes-away-at-age-91/)

**來源支持的事實與改寫界線**：Institute expresses great sadness and records Dr Goodall's death, October 1 2025; adverb deeply is newly written not a quote。此英文例句屬教學改寫，非原文引述。考點 deeply 實際包含於英文句中。

### 115-00-E-14｜too late

英文：A July 2026 UN report urged faster action so it would not be too late to make progress on global goals.

中文：2026年7月的聯合國報告敦促各國加速採取行動，以免實現全球目標的進展為時已晚。

來源：United Nations Statistics Division，2026-07-07；[The Sustainable Development Goals Report 2026](https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/report/2026/)

**來源支持的事實與改寫界線**：Report says progress insufficient and calls for decisive push; too late phrase is educational inference, not direct UN quotation。此英文例句屬教學改寫，非原文引述。考點 too late 實際包含於英文句中。

### 115-00-E-16｜eat out

英文：Demand to eat out during the summer holidays helped restaurant sales grow in Taiwan in July 2026.

中文：2026年7月暑假期間的外食需求增加，帶動臺灣餐館營業額成長。

來源：Ministry of Economic Affairs (Taiwan), Statistics Department，2026-08-25；[115年7月批發、零售及餐飲業營業額統計](https://mnscdn.moea.gov.tw/MNS/populace/news/News.aspx?kind=1&menu_id=40&news_id=123727)

**來源支持的事實與改寫界線**：MOEA states July restaurant revenues grew 0.8% year-on-year, partly from summer eating-out and gatherings。此英文例句屬教學改寫，非原文引述。考點 eat out 實際包含於英文句中。

### 115-00-E-18｜work experience

英文：A 2026 World Economic Forum article said that many employers value work experience when hiring staff.

中文：2026年世界經濟論壇文章指出，許多雇主在招募員工時重視工作經驗。

來源：World Economic Forum，2026-04-01；[Entry-level hiring is tougher than ever. How universities are adapting](https://www.weforum.org/stories/education-and-skills/universities-prepare-students-entry-level-jobs)

**來源支持的事實與改寫界線**：WEF article cites 81% of employers expecting to prioritize work experience in skills assessment。此英文例句屬教學改寫，非原文引述。考點 work experience 實際包含於英文句中。
