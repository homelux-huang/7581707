- 來源：[Met Office](https://www.metoffice.gov.uk/blog/2024/review-of-2024-multiple-records-broken-in-a-year-of-mixed-weather)，2024-12-23。
- 英文：England had a very wet September in 2024, with ten counties setting September rainfall records.
- 中文：英格蘭2024年9月非常多雨，其中十個郡創下各自的9月降雨紀錄。
- 查證範圍：Source reports ten English counties' wettest September, not a claim about all UK locations.
